    ABOUT

    FLUXUS [Art Archive & Design Studio] is an independent creative platform founded by Dongjin Choi, documenting experiments across graphic design, objects, digital media, and visual culture.

    Originally developed as a personal archive, FLUXUS brings together selected works, ongoing experiments, editions, and fragments from Dongjin’s practice.

    Moving between the physical and digital, the platform explores how images, objects, materials, light, and technology can become systems for experiencing ideas.

    Today, FLUXUS operates as an evolving archive and experimental studio — a place for works to be collected, developed, released, and revisited.
